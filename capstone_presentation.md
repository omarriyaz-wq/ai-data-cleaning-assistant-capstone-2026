### Slide 2: The Data Engineering Dilemma
* **The Reality**: Data preparation accounts for 60%–80% of a data science lifecycle.
* **The Promise**: Generative AI promises instant data munging, parsing, and pipeline scaffolding.
* **The Peril**: AI produces code that is *syntactically flawless* but *semantically treacherous*.
* **The Objective**:
  1. Construct a rigorous public-style benchmark dataset featuring 13 documented dirty patterns.
  2. Benchmark ChatGPT Zero-Shot vs. Gemini Prompt-Engineered vs. Human Ground Truth.
  3. Catalog subtle failure modes (The AI Data Cleaning Bug Taxonomy).
  4. Build and deploy an open-source, production-ready, auditable cleaning pipeline.

---

### Slide 3: The Benchmark Dataset Architecture
* **Dataset**: International E-Commerce Customer Analytics & Demographics (`1,260` raw records).
* **13 Documented Real-World Anomalies**:
  1. *Primary Key Dirty Whitespace & Nulls*: `' CUST-1042 '` vs `'CUST-1042'`.
  2. *Cultural Name Characters*: Hyphenated names (`Jean-Luc`), apostrophes (`O'Connor`), prefixes (`Dr.`).
  3. *Inconsistent International Dates*: `DD/MM/YYYY` vs `MM/DD/YYYY` vs `YYYY.MM.DD` vs `'yesterday'`.
  4. *Dirty Currency & Shorthand Strings*: `'$52,000'`, `'USD 75,000'`, `'120k'`, negative currencies.
  5. *Negative Transaction Semantics*: Genuine refunds (`-$85.00`) vs extreme typo outliers (`$9.99M`).
  6. *Extreme Numeric Corruptions*: Age `-39`, `195`, `999 yrs`.
  7. *Inconsistent Categoricals*: 16 raw variants for Gender, 22 variants for Country.
  8. *High Non-Critical Missingness*: 38% nulls in `phone_number` (The AI trap).

### Slide 5: Anatomy of a Disaster — Bug #1: The Silent `dropna()`
* **The AI Code**:
  ```python
  df.dropna(inplace=True)  # Generated by ChatGPT Zero-Shot
  ```
* **The Catastrophe**:
  * In real data, missingness is distributed across multiple optional fields (`phone_number`, `email`, `satisfaction_score`).
  * The intersection of complete rows retained **only 16 rows out of 1,260**.
  * **98.7% of the dataset was silently deleted** without throwing a runtime error or warning.
* **The Takeaway**: A data pipeline that finishes with 0 errors can still silently destroy your business data.

---

### Slide 6: Anatomy of Failure — Semantics, Dates & Names
* **Bug #2: Date Swap Flaw**:
  * AI parsed `05/04/2023` as May 4th instead of April 5th (`dayfirst=False`).
  * Inverted customer cohorts and distorted time-series analysis.
* **Bug #3: Overzealous Regex Text Mutilation**:
  * AI used `r'[^a-zA-Z\s]'` to clean names.
  * Mutilated cultural names: `O'Connor` &rarr; `OConnor`, `Jean-Luc` &rarr; `JeanLuc`.
* **Bug #4: Financial Refund Annihilation**:
  * AI applied `.abs()` to purchase amounts.
  * Erased genuine accounting returns, converting customer refunds into positive revenue!

---

### Slide 7: Responsible AI & Fairness Audit
* **Demographic Parity Check**:
  * Under ChatGPT Zero-Shot, **100% of Non-Binary customers were deleted** because this group had slightly higher missingness in optional phone numbers.
  * *Unconscious algorithmic bias through naive cleaning.*
* **Statistical Distribution Distortion**:
  * Blind global mean imputation collapsed income variance and distorted demographic subgroups.
* **The Ethical Mandate**:
  * Automated cleaning systems must implement **subgroup-aware imputation** (conditioning income on `Country` and `Age Group`) and track an imputation indicator column for auditability.

---

### Slide 8: The Solution — `AIDataCleaningAssistant` Architecture
* **Design Philosophy**: Defensive, Schema-Aware, Non-Destructive, Auditable.
* **Core Components**:
  1. *Key-Normalized Deduplication*: Strips and uppercases keys prior to deduplication.
  2. *Smart Name Preserver*: Protects hyphens, apostrophes, and accents.
  3. *Resilient Multi-Format Date Parser*: Cascading fallback with `dayfirst=True`.
  4. *Financial Currency Engine*: Preserves negative refund signs while parsing `$`, `€`, `k`.
  5. *Subgroup-Conditioned Imputer*: Prevents distribution collapse and demographic bias.
  6. *Transparent Audit Logging*: Emits a JSON summary tracking every dropped row and imputed value.

---

### Slide 9: Prompt Engineering Strategies That Actually Work
1. **Zero-Shot is Banned**: Never allow LLMs to write cleaning code without a schema contract.
2. **Schema & Nullability Constraints**: Explicitly specify which fields are nullable and must not trigger row dropping.
3. **Chain-of-Thought (CoT)**: Require the LLM to inspect semantics (e.g. *"Are negative numbers refunds or errors?"*) before producing code.
4. **Adversarial Self-Correction**: Run generated code through an LLM reviewer checking for `dropna()` and regex mutilation.

---

### Slide 10: Conclusion & The 3 Golden Rules
1. **Rule 1: Trust, but Defend**: Use AI for rapid code scaffolding, but never deploy it without automated schema contracts and statistical checks.
2. **Rule 2: Never Drop Globally**: Always mandate key-based dropna (`subset=['customer_id']`).
3. **Rule 3: Audit Everything**: Production pipelines must generate transparent before/after retention reports to ensure data lineage and ethical compliance.
